# Data & Content

## Context
This section explains the content and information needed for our project. It outlines the main types of data we will use, how the content will be organized, and how it will support the overall goals of the project.

## Content
* **Campus Food Locations:** Names, coordinates, price ratings ($ to $$$), hours, and menu highlights around the York Markham campus.
* **Game Challenge Data:** Trivia questions, mini-game parameters, and reward criteria associated with each dining spot.
* **User Session Data:** Saved high scores, unlocked food spots, and completed challenge markers.
